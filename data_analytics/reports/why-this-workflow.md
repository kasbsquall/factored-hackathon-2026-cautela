* Projection, central scenario: 280.8 agent-hours of intake handling per year avoided out of 493.5. This is an offline projection with labeled assumptions, not a measured saving.

## 7. Where the data is uniform or templated, and what that means

* Complaint types with a subcategory are near uniform: 5 types between 11,886 and 12,297 complaints (max/min 1.035, coefficient of variation 0.0136).
* SLA breach rate per complaint type stays between 18.1% and 20.6%.
* `sla_breached` does not follow resolution time: median 15.0 days when breached (n = 3,058) and 16.0 days when not (n = 12,305).
* Interactions by hour of day: max/min 1.029 across 24 hours (coefficient of variation 0.0062). Only the weekday pattern varies.
* The repeat-complainer flag agrees with the history in 333 of 10,086 flagged complaints; 2,315 of 67,095 complaints have an earlier one within 90 days.
* Unrecognized-charge complaints carry 1 distinct description text(s) over 12,297 rows.
* Call transcripts: 546 distinct texts over 171,321 rows, 42 distinct customer turns, 1 distinct detected intent value(s).
* Complaints linked to an interaction: 0.0% (0 / 67,095). No complaint names a transaction.
* Fraud label on transactions: 0.098% (4,316 / 4,425,008). Mexican customers' transactions in USD: 100.0% (2,216,431 / 2,216,431).
* Customers whose registration branch is missing from branches: 99.997% (149,995 / 150,000).
* Claimed-amount currency does not follow the customer's country: Argentina ARS 210, Argentina COP 182, Argentina MXN 212, Argentina USD 219, Colombia ARS 306, Colombia COP 303, Colombia MXN 297, Colombia USD 316, Mexico ARS 490, Mexico COP 527, Mexico MXN 525, Mexico USD 489.

What this means for the conclusions. Where the outcome metrics (SLA breach, escalation, resolution time, repeat contact) sit within a few tenths of a point across complaint types, countries and segments, they cannot rank workflows. The choice of unrecognized charges rests on what the data does support: it is the largest complaint type (tied within sampling error with the next one); complaint contacts have the lowest first-contact resolution in the contact center, with the caveat of section 2 that disputes cannot be tied to a contact reason; its facts can be checked against records the bank holds (the customer's own transactions, with merchant, channel, amount and date), which is what the agent's tools verify. The text fields are templated, so language understanding is evaluated on team-generated scenarios (`ml/reports/results.md`), and the savings above are projections that assume the offline rate carries over to real traffic.
